Sunny Classroom

Approved design system. Royal blue does the work, sunny yellow adds the cheer: a friendly learning app for students that still reads as a serious tool for teachers. Static reference preview; the live app follows it. Full rules and every contrast ratio are in MASTER.md.

Approved · implemented 2026-11-24

Palette

Royal blue for actions, sunny yellow as a fill with dark ink, cool slate neutrals, and four soft card colors. Card and chip text is 6.38:1 or higher; dots use the solid shade (3:1 or more on white).

Controls, chips and badges

Filled buttons sit on a 3px lip and press down into it. Chips, badges, search and toggles are pills. The focus ring is shown on the last button.
